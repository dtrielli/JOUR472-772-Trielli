# Suggested Core Datasets

This menu focuses on Maryland, Washington, D.C., and the broader DMV, along with federal datasets that can be narrowed to the region. It offers four options in each category. Check the source documentation and current coverage before choosing a dataset. Every project must also use at least one relevant supplementary dataset; see the [project instructions](data_analysis_project_intro.md#datasets).

## Maryland and local communities

- [Maryland foreclosure notices by census tract](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Housing/Maryland-Notices-of-Intent-to-Foreclose-by-Census-/nme2-wik5): compare notice patterns across communities; notices are not court foreclosure case records.
- [Maryland State Payments Data](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Budget/State-of-Maryland-Payments-Data-FY2008-to-FY2024/7syw-q4cy): examine payments by agency, vendor, purpose, or time; distinguish payments from budgets and contracts.
- [Maryland public school enrollment trends](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Education/Maryland-Public-School-Enrollment-Trends-Kindergar/9ju3-j8k6): compare enrollment by jurisdiction and grade band from 2014 to 2024.
- [Montgomery County food inspections, July 2024 onward](https://data.montgomerycountymd.gov/d/dkrp-gr48): explore inspection type, results, and location; learn how inspection fields and outcomes are defined.

## Environment and public health

- [Maryland reported sewer overflows](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Government/Reported-Sewer-Overflows-New-for-2023-/stgj-u72u): examine reported events by location, time, and overflow type; the reporting system changed in 2023.
- [Maryland Department of the Environment air complaints](https://opendata.maryland.gov/Energy-and-Environment/Maryland-Department-of-the-Environment-MDE-Air-and/crti-ybyp): look at complaint types, locations, and responses; complaints are not confirmed violations or a complete measure of pollution.
- [EPA Toxics Release Inventory (TRI)](https://www.epa.gov/toxics-release-inventory-tri-program/tri-basic-data-files-calendar-years-1987-present): study facility-reported chemical releases at Maryland and nearby facilities; review reporting thresholds and field definitions.
- [CDC PLACES](https://www.cdc.gov/places/tools/data-portal.html): compare modeled health estimates for Maryland, D.C., and nearby communities; small-area estimates are not direct counts.

## Transportation and infrastructure

- [Capital Bikeshare trip history](https://capitalbikeshare.com/system-data): investigate when, where, and by whom bikes are used across the DMV; trip records describe this system, not all regional travel.
- [NHTSA Fatality Analysis Reporting System (FARS)](https://www.nhtsa.gov/research-data/fatality-analysis-reporting-system-fars): analyze fatal crashes in Maryland and D.C.; FARS covers fatal crashes, not all collisions, and uses linked tables.
- [FCC National Broadband Map data](https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/data-download/nationwide-data): compare reported internet availability by provider, technology, speed, or location in the DMV; availability is not subscription or service quality.
- [Bureau of Transportation Statistics airline on-time data](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/ontime/): examine delays and cancellations at BWI, DCA, and IAD; narrow to selected airports, airlines, or time periods.

## Federal government, culture, and recreation

- [Federal Election Commission campaign finance data](https://www.fec.gov/data/browse-data/): examine contributions or spending in Maryland and D.C. federal races; transaction-level files can be large.
- [USAspending.gov awards data](https://www.usaspending.gov/): investigate federal contracts and grants connected to Maryland and D.C.; distinguish awards, obligations, and outlays.
- [National Park Service visitor use statistics](https://irma.nps.gov/Stats/): compare visits to DMV-area park units over time; check each park's counting methods and whether recent estimates are final.
- [Smithsonian Open Access collections](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/devtools): explore collection records at a federal cultural institution in D.C.; narrow the broad collection and check the rights status of individual items.

## Supplementary data

The [American Community Survey (ACS)](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data.html) can add population denominators or demographic and economic context. [Census geographic boundary files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html) can help match data by place. Align geography and time periods carefully, and do not treat correlation or geographic overlap as proof of causation.
